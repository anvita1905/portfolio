About Me

Rooted in India, I am a student, writer, editor, and creative whose work explores the intersections of politics, art, and culture through storytelling that is both reflective and bold.

My creative journey has been shaped by a deep curiosity for human expression, visual aesthetics, and the written word, a combination that drives me to turn emotion and observation into narrative. I believe in words that look as beautiful as they feel, and stories that make people pause.

I see creativity as both rebellion and reflection, a language of self-definition. My portfolio is not just a collection of work, but a continuous experiment in voice, design, and narrative honesty. Each piece, whether written, visual, or collaborative, carries the same intention: to make meaning feel visible, and to turn culture into conversation.
